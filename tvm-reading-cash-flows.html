Reading Cash Flows

Before any formula, learn to see the cash flows in a story. Four short activities. No calculations needed.

Activity 1

Match the story to its cash flows

Tap a story, then tap the timeline that shows its cash flows. When you get it right, the clue words light up and the formula appears.

Start by tapping a story.

Activity 2

Clue-word detective

Every cash-flow story answers four questions: how much, when does it start, how long, and is it constant or growing. Tap the words in the story that answer them. Words that don't matter will fade.

Activity 3

Build the annuity from two perpetuities

Where does the annuity formula on your sheet come from? Not from algebra. An annuity is simply a perpetuity that stops. Three steps, one guess at each.
